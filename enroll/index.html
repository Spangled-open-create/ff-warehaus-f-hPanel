The Challenge

Most product businesses grow
into logistics they weren't ready for.

Garage, spare room, rented unit — wherever you're storing inventory, it's costing more than it should and slowing your fulfillment down. Every square foot of improvised storage is margin leaving through the floor.

You don't know exactly what's on hand, what's moving, or when you'll run out — until it's already a problem. Reactive inventory management is one of the fastest ways to lose a customer you worked hard to acquire.

The unboxing experience is your brand's first physical handshake. Generic brown boxes leave money and loyalty on the table. Custom luxury packaging isn't a luxury — it's a retention mechanism.

Every hour packing and shipping is an hour not spent on product, marketing, or sales. The math stops working past a certain scale — and that moment arrives faster than most founders expect.
